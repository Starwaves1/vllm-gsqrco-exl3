   bit-exact: b11211's MMQ tile loaders round K-quant sub-block scales to half, and MMQ quantizes

   division may round either way; one flipped q in an outlier channel moved a row by 3-5e-3
   (box-scripts/p2diag3.py). The test inputs are now nudged one ulp off every tie.
