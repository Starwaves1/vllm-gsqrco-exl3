K3: owned int8 tensor-core kernel for Q4_K / IQ4_XS / IQ2_S at 9..64 activation rows
(lcpp_mul_mat_mma_k, plugin/vllm_gguf_plugin/csrc/lcpp_owned_mma_k.cu). MTP k=3 decode: 4 rows per
sequence, so c = 4 / 8 / 16 -> 16 / 32 / 64 rows. RTX 3090 350 W, bf16 X, real GGUF blocks, op
time incl. q8_1 quantize (no output cast: the kernel writes X's dtype), 10 calls per CUDA graph.
Timings from k3_bench.py (interleaved rounds, median, vendored MMQ in the same rounds) unless noted.

DRAM floor (floor.py: torch.sum over a buffer of the same bytes, same CUDA-graph timing):
  17408x5120  Q4_K 65.4 us (50.1 MB)  IQ4_XS 58.9 (47.3 MB)  IQ2_S 37.8 (28.5 MB)
  5120x17408  Q4_K 62.0               IQ4_XS 58.8            IQ2_S 37.8

  sustained read rate at 1 GiB: 888 GB/s (the "physical" floor: 56 / 53 / 32 us at 17408x5120)

Design (all iterations): CTA = 4 warps x 16 weight rows (one m16 mma tile per warp) x all columns;
per K step the raw GGUF blocks of the CTA's rows (exact 16 / 8 / 4-byte granules) and the
block_q8_1_mmq blocks of every column are cp.async'd into shared memory, 1-3 steps ahead; each
warp decodes its rows straight into A fragments and runs one m16n8k32 (IQ2_S: two m16n8k16, its
sub-scales are per 16 values) per 32-value slice and 8-column tile; per-slice scaling is the
vendored MMQ vec_dot expression; rows < 2 CTAs per SM get a K split summed in order by a second
kernel, otherwise the kernel writes X's dtype directly (no cast launch).
