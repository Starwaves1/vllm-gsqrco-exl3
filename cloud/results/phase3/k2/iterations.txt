The mma kernel does not extend cheaply: its per-warp activation staging (8 columns x 8 slices)
plus the 32 KB IQ3_S table already fill 2 CTAs/SM; 16/32 columns need the columns shared
across warps (warps splitting rows, not K) or a smaller table. Estimate from the instruction
mix (decode ~2/3 of the per-mma work, B + epilogue ~1/3 per 8 columns): ~107 us at n16 (MMQ
134), ~160 at n32 (MMQ 159), i.e. worth it at 16 rows only. Not built.
